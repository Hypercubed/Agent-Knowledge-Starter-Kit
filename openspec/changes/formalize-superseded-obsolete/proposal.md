## Why

Currently, the Agent Knowledge Starter Kit (AKSK) lacks a formal, fully-specified process for handling deprecated durable knowledge (like superseded decisions and obsolete troubleshooting entries) in its generated wiki indices. Simply removing these entries hurts discoverability of historical context, and frontmatter alone is insufficient since agents primarily navigate via the generated `index.md` files. OpenWiki's index generator renders only Files/Directories sections with no lifecycle support (verified against the installed `openwiki` dist), so the convention must survive regeneration instead of relying on hand-edited index sections.

## What Changes

We formalize the procedure for deprecating durable knowledge inside the AKSK, for the consolidated layout (curated pages under `openwiki/{decisions,troubleshooting}/`, indexes generated by `sync_wiki_indexes.mjs`). When a decision is superseded or a troubleshooting entry becomes obsolete:

1. Its frontmatter carries `aksk_status: superseded` (plus `aksk_superseded_by` pointing at the successor) on the curated wiki page - this is the machine-readable source of truth, matching the `learning-distill` frontmatter schemas.
2. Its frontmatter `description` carries a `[SUPERSEDED]` / `[OBSOLETE]` prefix, so the generated indexes surface the state without hand edits that regeneration would wipe.
3. The page body carries a status banner stating the deprecation and linking the successor - the human-readable lock.

The `docs-lint` stale-decision check enforces this convention against curated pages; there is no index-generator change in this change (upstream lifecycle rendering in OpenWiki is a separate option).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `deprecated-knowledge-convention`: Establish the frontmatter + description-prefix + body-banner convention for superseded and obsolete durable knowledge under generated OpenWiki indexes.

### Modified Capabilities

(None directly; the convention is verified by the cross-tool lint's stale-decision check rather than automated in an index generator.)

## Impact

- The schema reference for curated entries lives at `openwiki/maintenance-format.md` (migrated from the former `.agents/docs/MAINTENANCE.md`); its lifecycle vocabulary is reconciled to the `aksk_status` schemas and the convention is documented there.
- `.agents/skills/docs-lint/SKILL.md` verifies deprecation marking as part of its stale-decision check.
- `.agents/skills/learning-distill/references/*.schema.json` already carry `aksk_status` / `aksk_superseded_by`; no schema change needed.
